# 3.1.2 — Details content

## What was asked

Show the whole article on the details screen: image, title, byline, date, description, content, in
that scrollable order. Any section whose field is null is left out entirely rather than shown as an
empty box, and the API's truncation counter (e.g. `"... [+2114 chars]"`) is stripped off the end of
`content` so only the readable text remains.

## What changed

- `lib/features/news_details/news_details_screen.dart` — rewritten. Still a `StatelessWidget` with
  no cubit (that arrives in 3.1.3). The body is a scrollable `ListView`: a full-width
  `AppNetworkImage` inside a fixed 16:9 `AspectRatio` (so it reuses the shared placeholder/loading
  behaviour instead of duplicating it), then a padded column with title, byline, date, description,
  and content.
  - The byline joins `author` and `source.name` with the existing `AppStrings.sourceDateSeparator`
    (`' · '`) — the same separator the feed card already uses to join source and date — instead of
    adding a near-duplicate constant with the same value. It stands alone when `author` is null, and
    is left out completely (no blank row) when both are null, matching the source/date logic already
    proven in the feed card.
  - `content` goes through a private regex that strips a trailing run of dots/ellipsis followed by
    `[+N chars]`, so the counter is gone regardless of whether it's preceded by `...` or `…`.
- `test/features/news_details/news_details_screen_test.dart` — extended with: a full article renders
  all six sections (checked via the image's url, title, byline, date, description, and the
  already-stripped content text); an article with null `description`/`content` renders neither and
  the screen has exactly the three remaining `Text` widgets (title, byline, date); the truncation
  counter is confirmed gone (`find.textContaining('chars]')` finds nothing); a null `urlToImage`
  shows the shared placeholder; the byline shows the source alone when `author` is null; the byline
  is omitted entirely when both `author` and `source` are null.
