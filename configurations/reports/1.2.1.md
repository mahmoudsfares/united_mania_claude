# 1.2.1 — Colours and app theme

## What was asked

Define the app's red-and-white visual identity in one place: a colours file holding the red
primary, its accents, the surface and error colours, and white for text; and a theme file that
builds a `ThemeData` from those colours, with the whole text theme set to white.

## What changed

- `lib/core/utils/app_colors.dart` — new. Six constants: `primary` (the red, Manchester United's
  shade, `#DA291C`), `primaryLight` and `primaryDark` (its accents), `surface` (the red background
  colour), `error`, and `white`.
- `lib/core/utils/app_theme.dart` — new. `AppTheme.theme` builds a `ThemeData` whose `ColorScheme`
  takes `primary` / `secondary` / `surface` / `error` from those constants and white for every "on"
  colour, sets `scaffoldBackgroundColor` to the surface red, and applies white to every style in the
  text theme (not just the body style).

## What was tested

- `test/core/utils/app_theme_test.dart` — 3 tests, written first and confirmed failing before the
  two files above existed:
  - `colorScheme.primary` equals `AppColors.primary`.
  - The body text style's colour equals `AppColors.white`.
  - A widget test pumping a `MaterialApp` built from the theme, with a plain `Scaffold` and `Text`:
    the Scaffold's background resolves to the surface red, and the text's rendered colour resolves
    to white.
- `flutter analyze` — no issues found.
- `flutter test` — all 4 tests in `test/` passing (the pre-existing smoke test plus the 3 above).
- Checked by hand (grep across `lib/`) that no `Color(...)` or `Colors.*` literal exists anywhere
  outside `app_colors.dart`.

## Anything left open

Nothing for this subtask. `lib/main.dart` does not use this theme yet — wiring `AppTheme.theme`
into the `MaterialApp` happens in 2.3.2 together with routing, per the work breakdown's dependency
order.
